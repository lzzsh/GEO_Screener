Create ONE final production logo lockup, not a presentation board or mockup. A beautifully balanced, distinctive compact symbol above a precise wordmark "GEO Screener". No other text. Core concept: an abstract geometric uppercase G built from a broad cobalt-blue open circular ribbon, with a very simple selected pathway of three connected circular sample nodes integrated into the G's inner horizontal opening. The nodes follow a gentle ascending step sequence to suggest ordered protocol stages and source-to-result traceability. One small teal node is the selection accent. The negative space should remain open, elegant and unmistakably readable at small sizes. The scientific meaning comes from the sample nodes and sequence, not a generic elaborate DNA ornament. Style: masterfully constructed flat vector-like brand identity, consistent geometric proportions, rounded terminals, sharp clean edges, no fine details. Think credible computational biology research software, editorial clarity, enduring identity, not a hospital logo.
Palette: cobalt #2563EB, restrained teal #14B8A6, dark ink #16324F wordmark. Flat solid colors only, no gradients. Wordmark modern humanist/geometric sans-serif, medium-bold, excellent kerning, exact capitalization "GEO Screener", one line centered below mark. Symbol height roughly 1.6 times wordmark width's quarter; overall lockup centered and confidently sized. Make the symbol and typography feel custom and equally resolved.
Avoid hexagonal meshes, DNA helices, medical crosses, globes, magnifying glasses, checkmark clichés, shadows, 3D, glow, texture, thin network tangles, decorative sparkles, badges, slogans, borders, watermarks, small explanatory labels. Deliver the actual logo graphic only on clean pure white.
